# MotorShield Comprehensive — Policy Terms (2026)

## 1. Overview

MotorShield Comprehensive protects your vehicle against accidental damage, theft, fire and natural perils, and covers your legal liability to third parties. The vehicle is insured on an **agreed value** basis stated in the policy schedule.

## 2. What Is Covered

1. **Accidental damage** to your vehicle, including collision and overturning.
2. **Fire, lightning and explosion.**
3. **Theft** of the vehicle or parts of the vehicle (following forced entry).
4. **Natural perils:** flood, cyclone, storm, landslide and earthquake.
5. **Third-party liability:**
   - Death or bodily injury to third parties — as required by the Motor Traffic Act.
   - Third-party property damage — up to **LKR 1,000,000** per accident.
6. **Strike, riot, civil commotion and terrorism (SRCC/T)** — provided through the national SRCC & T Fund and charged separately on the premium.

## 3. Excess (Amount You Pay per Claim)

| Situation | Excess per claim |
|---|---|
| Standard excess | LKR 15,000 |
| Driver under 25 years of age, or holding a driving licence for less than 2 years | LKR 30,000 (replaces the standard excess) |
| Windscreen-only claim with Windscreen Add-on | No excess |

## 4. No Claim Bonus (NCB)

A discount on your renewal premium for each consecutive claim-free year.

| Claim-free years | NCB discount |
|---|---|
| 1 year | 20% |
| 2 years | 25% |
| 3 years | 30% |
| 4 years | 35% |
| 5 years or more | 40% (maximum) |

If you make an own-damage claim, your NCB **drops to 0%** at the next renewal, unless you have the **NCB Protector** add-on. Third-party-only claims where the other party is at fault and pays in full do not affect your NCB.

## 5. Optional Add-ons

| Add-on | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Windscreen Cover | Windscreen and window glass replacement up to LKR 150,000 per year, without affecting NCB |
| Roadside Assistance | 24/7 towing up to 50 km, battery jump-start, flat tyre change, fuel delivery |
| NCB Protector | Keeps your NCB after one own-damage claim per policy year |
| Personal Accident (PA) | LKR 500,000 per seat for death or permanent disability of driver and passengers |

Add-on prices are listed in `motor_premium_table_2026.md`.

## 6. Total Loss

The vehicle is treated as a **total loss** if the cost of repair is more than **75% of the agreed value**. In a total loss, we pay the agreed value minus the excess, and the salvage becomes the property of Ceylon Shield.

## 7. What Is NOT Covered (Exclusions)

1. Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
2. Driving without a valid driving licence for that vehicle class.
3. Using a private vehicle **for hire or reward**, including ride-hailing or delivery apps, unless a **Hiring Endorsement** has been added to the policy.
4. Wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown.
5. Damage to tyres, unless the vehicle is damaged in the same accident.
6. Racing, speed testing or rallies.
7. Driving outside Sri Lanka.
8. Consequential loss (e.g., loss of income while the vehicle is being repaired).
