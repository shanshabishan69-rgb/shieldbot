# MotorShield Third Party — Policy Terms (2026)

## 1. Overview

MotorShield Third Party is the **minimum cover required by law** under the Motor Traffic Act of Sri Lanka. It covers your legal liability to other people. **It does not cover damage to your own vehicle.**

## 2. What Is Covered

| Cover | Limit |
|---|---|
| Death or bodily injury to third parties | As required by the Motor Traffic Act |
| Damage to third-party property | Up to **LKR 500,000** per accident |

## 3. What Is NOT Covered

1. Any damage to, or theft of, **your own vehicle**.
2. Injury to the driver of the insured vehicle.
3. Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
4. Driving without a valid licence.
5. Using a private vehicle for hire or reward without a Hiring Endorsement.

## 4. Other Terms

- **No Claim Bonus (NCB) does not apply** to Third Party policies.
- There is **no excess** on Third Party claims.
- You may upgrade to MotorShield Comprehensive at any time; the vehicle must be inspected before the upgrade.

Third Party premiums are fixed by vehicle type. See `motor_premium_table_2026.md`.
