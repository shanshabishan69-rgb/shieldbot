# Motor Premium Rates (2026)

All premiums below are **annual**, **before No Claim Bonus**, and **exclude government taxes and the SRCC & T Fund levy**.

## 1. MotorShield Comprehensive — Rate as % of Agreed Vehicle Value

| Vehicle Type | Up to LKR 5M | LKR 5M – 10M | LKR 10M – 20M | Above LKR 20M |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private car | 3.00% | 2.75% | 2.50% | 2.25% |
| SUV / Jeep | 3.25% | 3.00% | 2.75% | 2.50% |
| Dual purpose / Van | 3.50% | 3.25% | 3.00% | Not offered |
| Motorcycle | 4.00% | 3.50% | Not offered | Not offered |
| Three-wheeler (private use) | 4.50% | Not offered | Not offered | Not offered |

**Minimum premium:** LKR 25,000 for cars, SUVs and vans; LKR 8,000 for motorcycles and three-wheelers.

**Example:** A private car with an agreed value of LKR 8,000,000 falls in the LKR 5M – 10M band → 2.75% × 8,000,000 = **LKR 220,000** per year (before NCB and taxes).
