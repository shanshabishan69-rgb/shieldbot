# Home Protect — Policy Terms (2026)

## 1. Overview

Home Protect insures your **building**, your **contents** (household items), or both, against damage and loss.

## 2. What Is Covered

1. Fire, lightning and explosion.
2. Flood, storm and cyclone.
3. Landslide — **only if** the property is not in a high-risk zone identified by the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO).
4. Earthquake.
5. Burglary and theft — **only with forced and violent entry** into the home.
6. Burst water pipes and overflowing water tanks.
7. Accidental damage to fixed glass.

## 3. Limits on Contents

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Any single item (unless declared separately) | LKR 250,000 |
| Jewellery and valuables | Up to 10% of the contents sum insured, **only if kept in a locked safe** |
| Cash and currency notes | **Not covered** |
| Laptops and mobile phones outside the home | Not covered (home only) |

Items worth more than LKR 250,000 must be listed in the policy schedule to be covered in full.

## 4. Excess

| Claim Type | Excess per Claim |
|---|---|
| Standard | LKR 10,000 |
| Flood | LKR 25,000 |

## 5. Important Conditions

- **Unoccupied homes:** If the home is left unoccupied for more than **45 consecutive days**, burglary and theft cover is **suspended** unless you inform us in advance.
- **Underinsurance (Average Clause):** If the sum insured is less than **85%** of the actual value of the property, any claim will be reduced in proportion to the underinsurance.
- Burglary must be reported to the police within **24 hours**.

## 6. Exclusions

1. Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, termites and vermin.
2. Theft without forced entry, or theft by family members or domestic staff.
3. Loss or damage while the home is let to tenants, unless declared.
4. Damage from poor construction or unauthorised building work.
5. War and nuclear risks.

## 7. Premium Rates

| Cover | Annual Rate |
|---|---|
| Building | 0.12% of the building sum insured |
| Contents | 0.35% of the contents sum insured |
| Minimum premium | LKR 7,500 |
