# Contact Channels and Service Hours

## Hotlines

| Purpose | Number | Available |
|---|---|---|
| General customer hotline | 0117 000 111 | 24 hours, 7 days |
| Motor accident & claims hotline | 0117 000 222 | 24 hours, 7 days |
| Travel emergency assistance (from overseas) | +94 117 000 333 | 24 hours, 7 days |
| WhatsApp (text only) | 077 000 1111 | 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily |

## Email

| Purpose | Email |
|---|---|
| General enquiries | info@ceylonshield.lk |
| Claims | claims@ceylonshield.lk |
| Complaints (Customer Care Unit) | care@ceylonshield.lk |

Emails are answered within **2 working days**.

## Shield App

Available on Android and iOS. Claims can be submitted through the app 24/7. Document uploads must be in PDF, JPG or PNG format and no larger than 10 MB per file.

## Important

- Ceylon Shield staff will **never** ask for your card PIN, OTP or online banking password.
- Do not pay premiums into a personal bank account of an agent. Official payment methods are listed in `premium_payment_methods.md`.
