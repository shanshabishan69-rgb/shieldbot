# Ceylon Shield Insurance PLC — Company Overview

## 1. Who We Are

Ceylon Shield Insurance PLC is a Sri Lankan insurance company founded in **1998** and headquartered at **No. 245, Galle Road, Colombo 03**. We are licensed by the **Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL)** to carry out both **General Insurance** and **Life Insurance** business.

Today we serve **more than 450,000 policyholders** through **8 branches** and a network of over **1,200 registered insurance agents** island-wide.

## 2. Our Vision and Mission

- **Vision:** To be Sri Lanka's most trusted protector of families, vehicles and homes.
- **Mission:** To provide simple, fair and fast insurance, and to settle every genuine claim on time.

## 3. Our Products

| Category | Product Name | Plans / Variants |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | MotorShield Comprehensive | One plan with optional add-ons |
| Motor | MotorShield Third Party | Legal minimum cover |
| Health | Health Shield Plus | Silver, Gold, Platinum |
| Life | Life Secure | Term Protect, Smart Saver |
| Home | Home Protect | Building, Contents, or both |
| Travel | Travel Guard | Asia, Worldwide (excl. USA/Canada), Worldwide (incl. USA/Canada) |

We do **not** currently offer Takaful (Islamic insurance), agricultural insurance, or commercial fleet insurance for more than 10 vehicles.

## 4. Digital Services

The **Shield App** (Android and iOS) allows customers to:
- View policies and download digital insurance cards (e-cards)
- Pay premiums by card at no extra charge
- Submit claims and upload documents
- Track claim status using the claim reference number

## 5. Customer Commitment

We aim to answer every call to our hotline within **60 seconds** and to resolve complaints within **21 working days**. For details on how to raise a complaint, see `complaints_and_grievance_procedure.md`.
