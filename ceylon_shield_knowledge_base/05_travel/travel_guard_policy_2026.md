# Travel Guard — Policy Terms (2026)

## 1. Overview

Travel Guard covers medical emergencies and travel problems for Sri Lankan residents travelling overseas.

## 2. Plans and Regions

| Plan | Countries Covered |
|---|---|
| Asia | All Asian countries **except Japan** |
| Worldwide (excl. USA/Canada) | All countries except the USA and Canada (**includes Japan**) |
| Worldwide (incl. USA/Canada) | All countries |

## 3. Benefits (Limits in USD)

| Benefit | Asia | Worldwide (excl. USA/Canada) | Worldwide (incl. USA/Canada) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency medical expenses | 50,000 | 100,000 | 250,000 |
| Emergency evacuation | 25,000 | 50,000 | 100,000 |
| Trip cancellation | 1,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 |
| Lost baggage (max USD 300 per item) | 1,000 | 1,500 | 1,500 |
| Baggage delay | USD 100 for every 12 hours of delay, up to USD 400 | Same | Same |
| Loss of passport | 300 | 300 | 300 |
| Personal liability | 25,000 | 50,000 | 100,000 |

Baggage delay is paid only when the delay is **more than 12 hours** after arrival, and receipts for essential purchases must be provided.

## 4. Key Conditions

1. **The policy must be bought before departure from Sri Lanka.** It cannot be bought or extended while you are already overseas.
2. **Single trip:** maximum trip length of **90 days**.
3. **Annual multi-trip:** unlimited trips in 12 months, maximum **45 days per trip**.
4. **Age limit:** up to 75 years. Travellers aged **70 and above** have medical expense limits **halved**.
5. Cover starts when you leave Sri Lanka. Trip cancellation cover starts on the date of purchase.
6. Trip cancellation is covered only for: serious illness, injury or death of the traveller or a close family member; visa rejection (with embassy letter); or natural disasters at the destination.

## 5. Exclusions

1. Pre-existing medical conditions.
2. Adventure sports (scuba diving, skydiving, mountaineering above 4,000 m) unless the **Adventure Add-on** is purchased.
3. Travelling against medical advice or to receive medical treatment.
4. Travel to countries under a Sri Lankan government travel ban.
5. Losses from leaving baggage unattended in a public place.

## 6. Emergencies Abroad

Call **+94 117 000 333** (24/7) **before** visiting a hospital wherever possible. For claims after you return, see **Claims Overview — Section 4**.
