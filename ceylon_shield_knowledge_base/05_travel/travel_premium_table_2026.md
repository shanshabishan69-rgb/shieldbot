# Travel Guard — Premiums per Traveller (2026)

All amounts are in **LKR** per traveller.

| Trip Length | Asia | Worldwide (excl. USA/Canada) | Worldwide (incl. USA/Canada) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 – 7 days | 4,800 | 7,500 | 11,000 |
| 8 – 15 days | 7,200 | 11,800 | 17,500 |
| 16 – 30 days | 11,500 | 18,900 | 27,800 |
| 31 – 60 days | 19,800 | 32,000 | 46,500 |
| 61 – 90 days | 26,500 | 43,500 | 63,000 |
| Annual multi-trip | 38,000 | 62,000 | 89,000 |

## Loadings and Add-ons

- **Age 66 – 75:** +50% loading on the premium.
- **Adventure Add-on:** +25% of the premium.
- **Family plan** (2 adults + up to 3 children under 18): 2.5 × the single adult premium.
