# Health Shield Plus — Annual Premiums per Person (2026)

All amounts are in **LKR** and are **annual premiums per person**, based on age at the policy start date.

| Age Band | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 – 30 | 28,000 | 46,000 | 82,000 |
| 31 – 40 | 34,000 | 55,000 | 98,000 |
| 41 – 50 | 45,000 | 72,000 | 125,000 |
| 51 – 60 | 62,000 | 98,000 | 168,000 |
| 61 – 65 | 85,000 | 135,000 | 230,000 |
| Dependent child (3 months – 21 years) | 15,000 | 24,000 | 42,000 |

## Discounts and Loadings

- **Family discount:** 10% off the total premium when 3 or more members are on the same policy.
- **Monthly payment loading:** paying monthly adds 5% to the annual premium.
- After age 65, renewal premiums follow the 61–65 band plus 8% for each additional year of age.
