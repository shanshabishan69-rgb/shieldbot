# Health Shield Plus — Policy Terms (2026 Edition)

> This edition applies to all policies issued or renewed on or after **1 January 2026**. It replaces the 2025 edition.

## 1. Overview

Health Shield Plus pays for in-patient hospital treatment in Sri Lanka and, on selected plans, out-patient treatment and overseas treatment.

## 2. What's New in 2026

- Classic and Premier plans are replaced by **Silver, Gold and Platinum**.
- Pre-existing condition waiting period **reduced from 24 months to 12 months**.
- Non-network co-payment **reduced from 20% to 10%**.
- **Children can now be added as dependents.**
- Out-patient (OPD) cover added to Gold and Platinum.

## 3. Plans and Benefits

| Benefit | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual limit per person | LKR 1,000,000 | LKR 2,000,000 | LKR 4,000,000 |
| Room limit per day | LKR 15,000 | LKR 25,000 | LKR 40,000 |
| ICU per day | LKR 30,000 | LKR 50,000 | LKR 80,000 |
| Out-patient (OPD) per year | Not covered | LKR 50,000 | LKR 100,000 |
| Maternity | Not covered | LKR 150,000 | LKR 250,000 |
| Overseas treatment (India / Singapore) | Not covered | Not covered | Up to LKR 2,000,000 with pre-approval |
| Ambulance per admission | LKR 5,000 | LKR 10,000 | LKR 15,000 |

## 4. Waiting Periods

| Type | Waiting Period |
|---|---|
| General illness | 30 days from policy start |
| Accidents | No waiting period — covered from day 1 |
| Pre-existing conditions | 12 months |
| Maternity (Gold and Platinum only) | 9 months |
| Specified surgeries (cataract, hernia, knee replacement, tonsillectomy) | 6 months |

## 5. Co-payment

| Hospital Type | Co-payment |
|---|---|
| Network hospital (cashless) | 0% |
| Non-network hospital (reimbursement) | 10% of the eligible bill |

Ceylon Shield has a network of **45+ private hospitals** across Sri Lanka. The latest list is available in the Shield App.

## 6. Eligibility

- Main member entry age: **18 to 65 years**.
- Dependent children: **3 months to 21 years**.
- Renewal is guaranteed up to age **75** if premiums are paid without a break.

## 7. Exclusions

1. Cosmetic or aesthetic surgery.
2. Dental treatment, unless required because of an accident.
3. Spectacles, contact lenses and hearing aids.
4. Infertility and assisted reproduction treatment.
5. Experimental or unproven treatment.
6. Injuries from hazardous sports, unless declared and accepted.
7. Treatment related to alcohol or drug abuse.
8. War and nuclear risks.

## 8. Claims

For cashless and reimbursement claims, see `health_claims_procedure.md`. Time limits are listed in **Claims Overview — Section 4**.
