# Health Shield Plus — Policy Terms (2025 Edition)

> This edition applied to policies issued or renewed between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2025.

## 1. Overview

Health Shield Plus is a hospitalisation insurance plan that pays for in-patient treatment at private and government hospitals in Sri Lanka.

## 2. Plans

| Plan | Annual Limit per Person | Room Limit per Day | Out-patient (OPD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic | LKR 750,000 | LKR 12,000 | Not covered |
| Premier | LKR 1,500,000 | LKR 15,000 | Not covered |

## 3. Waiting Periods

| Type | Waiting Period |
|---|---|
| General illness | 30 days from policy start |
| Pre-existing conditions | 24 months |
| Maternity | 12 months (Premier plan only) |
| Specified surgeries (cataract, hernia, knee replacement, tonsillectomy) | 12 months |

## 4. Co-payment

Treatment at **non-network** hospitals carries a **20% co-payment**. No co-payment at network hospitals.

## 5. Eligibility

- Entry age: 18 to 60 years.
- Children cannot be added as dependents under the 2025 edition.

## 6. Exclusions

Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment, spectacles and contact lenses, infertility treatment, experimental treatment, injuries from hazardous sports, alcohol or drug abuse, and war.
