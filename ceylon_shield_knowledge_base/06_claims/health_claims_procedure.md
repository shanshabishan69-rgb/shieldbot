# Health Claims Procedure

## 1. Cashless Claims (Network Hospitals)

1. Show your **digital insurance card (e-card)** from the Shield App, together with your NIC, at the hospital admissions desk.
2. The hospital sends a pre-authorisation request to Ceylon Shield.
3. **Approval time:** within **2 hours** between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, and within **4 hours** outside these hours.
4. At discharge, you pay only the items that are not covered (for example, amounts above your room limit).

For **planned** admissions, the pre-authorisation must be requested at least **48 hours before** admission.

## 2. Reimbursement Claims (Non-network Hospitals)

1. Pay the hospital bill yourself.
2. Submit your claim within **30 days of discharge** through the Shield App, by email, or at a branch.
3. A **10% co-payment** applies to the eligible bill.

## 3. Out-patient (OPD) Claims — Gold and Platinum Only

- Upload the doctor's prescription and receipts in the Shield App within **60 days** of the treatment date.
- Pharmacy bills must show the prescription date and the doctor's name.

## 4. Overseas Treatment — Platinum Only

Pre-approval must be obtained **before travelling**. Treatment without pre-approval will not be reimbursed.

## 5. Common Reasons for Delay

- Missing original bills or discharge summary.
- Treatment during a waiting period.
- Room charges above the plan limit (the extra amount is not paid).

Required documents are listed in `claim_documents_checklist.md`.
