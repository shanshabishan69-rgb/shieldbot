# Claim Documents Checklist

✅ = required  ◻️ = required only in some cases (see notes)  — = not required

| Document | Motor Accident | Motor Theft | Health (Reimbursement) | Life Death | Home | Travel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Completed claim form | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| NIC of policyholder / claimant | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Driving licence | ✅ | — | — | — | — | — |
| Revenue licence and vehicle registration (CR) | ✅ | ✅ | — | — | — | — |
| Police report | ◻️ (1) | ✅ | — | ◻️ (2) | ◻️ (3) | ◻️ (4) |
| Photos of damage | ✅ | — | — | — | ✅ | ◻️ |
| Repair estimate | ✅ | — | — | — | ✅ | — |
| Police non-recovery certificate | — | ✅ | — | — | — | — |
| Original hospital bills and discharge summary | — | — | ✅ | — | — | ◻️ (5) |
| Death certificate | — | — | — | ✅ | — | — |
| Passport copy and boarding passes | — | — | — | — | — | ✅ |
| Airline Property Irregularity Report (PIR) | — | — | — | — | — | ◻️ (6) |
| Bank account details | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

**Notes**
1. Required for injury, theft, third-party property damage above LKR 25,000, or when the other driver refuses to give details.
2. Required for accidental or unnatural death.
3. Required for burglary and theft.
4. Required for theft or loss of passport or baggage overseas.
5. Required for medical claims.
6. Required for lost or delayed baggage.
