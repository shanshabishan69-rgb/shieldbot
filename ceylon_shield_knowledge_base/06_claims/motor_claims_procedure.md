# Motor Claims Procedure

## Step 1 — At the Accident Scene

1. Make sure everyone is safe. Call **1990** for an ambulance if anyone is injured.
2. Do **not** move the vehicle unless it is blocking traffic or the police allow it.
3. Call the **Motor Claims Hotline 0117 000 222** (24/7) and give your vehicle number and location.
4. Take photos of all vehicles, the damage, number plates and the road.
5. **Do not admit fault** or agree to pay anyone. Liability is decided by Ceylon Shield.

## Step 2 — On-the-spot Assessment

An assessor will come to the accident location:
- Within **4 hours** in the Western Province.
- Within **6 hours** in all other provinces.

If the assessor cannot reach you, the hotline will tell you how to take the vehicle to the nearest approved garage.

## Step 3 — Police Report

A police report is **required** when:
- A third party is injured or killed.
- The vehicle is stolen.
- Third-party property damage is more than LKR 25,000.
- The other driver refuses to give their details.

## Step 4 — Repairs

| Option | How It Works |
|---|---|
| Shield-approved garage (cashless) | We pay the garage directly. You pay only the excess. |
| Your own garage (reimbursement) | Submit a repair estimate first. Repairs must be approved before work starts. We reimburse the approved amount minus the excess. |

## Step 5 — Vehicle Theft

1. Report the theft to the police **within 24 hours**, and call the hotline.
2. If the vehicle is **not recovered within 60 days**, submit the police **non-recovery certificate**.
3. The claim is then settled at the agreed value minus the excess.

## Step 6 — Total Loss

If repair costs are more than **75% of the agreed value**, the vehicle is a total loss. See Section 6 of `motorshield_comprehensive_policy_2026.md`.

Required documents are listed in `claim_documents_checklist.md`.
