# Claim Settlement Timelines

The timeline starts **only after all required documents have been received**.

| Claim Type | Ceylon Shield Commitment |
|---|---|
| Motor — on-the-spot assessment | Within 4 hours (Western Province) / 6 hours (other provinces) |
| Motor — claim settlement | 10 working days |
| Motor — theft | After 60 days if not recovered, then 10 working days |
| Health — cashless approval | 2 hours (8 AM – 8 PM), 4 hours (other times) |
| Health — reimbursement | 14 working days |
| Health — OPD | 7 working days |
| Life — death claim | 30 days |
| Life — maturity | 7 working days from the maturity date |
| Life — critical illness | 21 working days |
| Home | 21 working days |
| Travel | 15 working days |

If a settlement is delayed beyond these timelines, you may escalate to the Customer Care Unit. See `complaints_and_grievance_procedure.md`.
