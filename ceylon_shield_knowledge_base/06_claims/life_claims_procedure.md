# Life Claims Procedure

## 1. Death Claims

1. The beneficiary (nominee) should notify Ceylon Shield within **90 days** of the date of death.
2. Submit the following documents:
   - Original death certificate
   - Original policy document
   - Claimant's NIC
   - Claimant's bank account details
   - Medical or post-mortem report, **if death occurred within 2 years** of the policy start date (contestability period)
3. Ceylon Shield settles the claim within **30 days** of receiving all documents.

If no beneficiary was named, the benefit is paid to the legal heirs on production of a court order or letters of administration.

## 2. Maturity Claims (Smart Saver)

- Maturity benefits are paid **automatically** within **7 working days** of the maturity date.
- Make sure your bank details in the Shield App are up to date at least one month before maturity.

## 3. Critical Illness Claims

1. Notify us within **90 days** of the diagnosis.
2. The diagnosis must be confirmed by a **registered specialist** with supporting test reports.
3. The insured person must survive **30 days** after the diagnosis (survival period).
4. CI claims are not payable for illnesses diagnosed within the first **90 days** of the policy.
