# Claims Overview and General Procedure

## Section 1 — Purpose

This document explains how to notify Ceylon Shield of a claim, the time limits for each product, and how claims are assessed, settled and appealed. Product-specific steps are in the motor, health and life claims procedures.

## Section 2 — How to Notify a Claim

You can notify a claim through any of these channels:
1. **Hotline:** 0117 000 111 (all products) or 0117 000 222 (motor accidents), 24/7.
2. **Shield App:** Claims → New Claim.
3. **Email:** claims@ceylonshield.lk
4. **Any branch** during opening hours (see `branch_directory.md`).

## Section 3 — Claim Reference Number

Every claim receives a reference number in the format **CS-CLM-YYYY-NNNNN** (for example, CS-CLM-2026-04821). Keep this number for all follow-ups.

## Section 4 — Notification Time Limits

| Product | First Notification | Full Claim Submission |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | Call the hotline within **24 hours** of the accident | Claim form within **7 days** |
| Health — planned admission | Pre-authorisation at least **48 hours before** admission | — |
| Health — emergency admission | Within **24 hours** of admission | — |
| Health — reimbursement | — | Within **30 days** of discharge |
| Health — OPD (Gold/Platinum) | — | Within **60 days** of the treatment date |
| Life — death or critical illness | Within **90 days** of the date of death or diagnosis | Documents as soon as possible |
| Home | Within **7 days** of the incident (burglary: police report within **24 hours**) | Claim form within **30 days** |
| Travel | Emergency assistance call before treatment where possible | Within **30 days** of returning to Sri Lanka |

Claims notified after these time limits **may be rejected**, unless there was a reasonable cause for the delay (for example, hospitalisation of the claimant).

## Section 5 — Assessment

1. A claims officer checks that the policy is active and that the loss is covered.
2. An assessor or medical officer may inspect the damage or review medical records.
3. Additional documents may be requested. The settlement timeline starts only when **all required documents** have been received.

## Section 6 — Settlement

- From **1 April 2026**, all claims are paid **by bank transfer only** to the policyholder's or beneficiary's bank account. Cheques are no longer issued.
- Settlement timelines are listed in `settlement_timelines.md`.

## Section 7 — Rejected Claims and Appeals

1. If a claim is rejected, we will give the reason in writing.
2. You may appeal to the **Claims Review Committee** within **30 days** of the rejection letter by emailing claims@ceylonshield.lk.
3. If you are still not satisfied, follow `complaints_and_grievance_procedure.md`.
