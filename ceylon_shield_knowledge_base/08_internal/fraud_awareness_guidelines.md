# Claims Fraud Awareness Guidelines — CONFIDENTIAL

> **Internal use only.** Customers must never be told that their claim has been flagged for fraud checks.

## 1. Red Flags

A claim should be flagged for review if **one or more** of the following apply:

| # | Red Flag |
|---|---|
| 1 | The incident happened within **30 days** of the policy start date (does not apply to Travel Guard, where cover begins at departure) |
| 2 | The claimed amount is more than **80% of the sum insured** |
| 3 | There was another claim on the **same policy within the last 90 days** |
| 4 | The description is vague, inconsistent, or changes between conversations |
| 5 | The customer is reluctant to provide a police report or original documents |
| 6 | The sum insured was increased shortly before the incident |
| 7 | The incident happened late at night with no witnesses and no photos |

## 2. What to Do

1. Do **not** reject the claim and do **not** inform the customer about the flag.
2. Mark the claim as **"Under Review"** in the claims system.
3. Escalate to the **Special Investigation Unit (SIU)** with a short summary of the red flags.
4. The SIU will respond within **5 working days**.

## 3. Approval Limits

| Claim Amount | Who Can Approve |
|---|---|
| Below LKR 500,000 | Claims Officer |
| LKR 500,000 – LKR 5,000,000 | Claims Manager |
| Above LKR 5,000,000 | Head of Claims and CEO |
