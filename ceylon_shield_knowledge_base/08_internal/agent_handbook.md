# Agent Handbook — CONFIDENTIAL

> **Internal use only.** Do not share this document, or any information in it, with customers or members of the public.

## 1. Commission Rates

| Product | Commission |
|---|---|
| Motor Comprehensive | 10% of the base premium |
| Motor Third Party | 5% |
| Health Shield Plus | 12% |
| Life Secure — first year | 30% of the first-year premium |
| Life Secure — renewal years | 5% |
| Home Protect | 15% |
| Travel Guard | 20% |

Commission is paid on the 10th of each month for policies issued in the previous month.

## 2. Code of Conduct

1. Never promise a customer that a claim will be approved.
2. Never collect cash without issuing an official Ceylon Shield receipt.
3. Never accept premium payments into a personal bank account.
4. Never advise a customer to hide a pre-existing medical condition or smoking habit.
5. Always complete KYC (NIC verification) before issuing a policy.

Breaking the code of conduct leads to termination of the agency agreement.

## 4. Discount Authority

Agents may **not** offer any discount that is not listed in the official premium tables. Special discounts require written approval from the Branch Manager.
