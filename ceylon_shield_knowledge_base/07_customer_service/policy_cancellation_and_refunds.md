# Policy Cancellation and Refunds

## 1. Free-look Period (Life and Health)

| Product | Free-look Period | Refund |
|---|---|---|
| Life Secure | 21 days from receiving the policy document | Full premium, minus the cost of any medical examination |
| Health Shield Plus | 14 days from receiving the policy document | Full premium, if no claim has been made |

## 2. Cancelling Motor and Home Policies (Short-period Scale)

You can cancel at any time. Ceylon Shield **keeps** part of the annual premium based on how long the policy was in force, and refunds the rest.

| Period the Policy Was in Force | Premium Kept by Ceylon Shield | Refund to You |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 1 month | 20% | 80% |
| Up to 3 months | 40% | 60% |
| Up to 6 months | 70% | 30% |
| Up to 9 months | 85% | 15% |
| More than 9 months | 100% | No refund |

**No refund** is given if a claim has been made during the current policy year.

## 3. Cancelling Travel Guard

- **Before departure:** full refund minus an administration fee of LKR 1,000.
- **After departure:** no refund.

## 4. Cancelling Health After the Free-look Period

Refunds follow the short-period scale in Section 2, provided no claim has been made.

## 5. How Refunds Are Paid

Refunds are paid by **bank transfer within 14 working days** of the cancellation request.
