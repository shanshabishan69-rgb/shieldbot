# Premium Payment Methods and Grace Periods

## 1. Ways to Pay

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| Shield App (credit/debit card) | No extra fee |
| Bank transfer | To the Ceylon Shield collection account only. Use your policy number as the reference. |
| Standing order | Available for monthly Life and Health premiums |
| Cash at any branch | Up to LKR 1,000,000 per transaction. Always collect an official receipt. |

Never pay premiums into an agent's personal account.

## 2. Payment Frequency

| Product | Options |
|---|---|
| Motor, Home, Travel | Annual or single payment only |
| Health | Annual or monthly (monthly adds a 5% loading) |
| Life | Annual, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly |

## 3. Grace Periods

| Product | Grace Period | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| Life | 30 days | The policy stays in force during the grace period. After that, it lapses. |
| Health | 15 days | Claims for treatment during the grace period are paid only after the overdue premium is received. |
| Motor, Home, Travel | **No grace period** | Cover starts only after the premium is paid. |

## 4. Reinstating a Lapsed Life Policy

A lapsed Life policy can be reinstated within **2 years** by paying all overdue premiums plus interest and providing a health declaration.
