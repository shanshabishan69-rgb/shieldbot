# Complaints and Grievance Procedure

We take every complaint seriously. Follow these levels in order.

## Level 1 — Branch or Agent

Raise the complaint with your agent or the **Branch Manager**. You will receive an acknowledgement within **2 working days**.

## Level 2 — Customer Care Unit

If the issue is not resolved at Level 1, contact the **Customer Care Unit**:
- Email: **care@ceylonshield.lk**
- Hotline: **0117 000 111**
- Letter: Customer Care Unit, Ceylon Shield Insurance PLC, No. 245, Galle Road, Colombo 03

| Stage | Timeline |
|---|---|
| First response | Within **7 working days** |
| Final resolution | Within **21 working days** |

## Level 3 — External Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our final response, you may contact:
1. **The Insurance Ombudsman** — an independent service, free of charge for policyholders.
2. **The Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL).**

## What to Include in Your Complaint

- Your full name and NIC number
- Policy number and, if relevant, claim reference number (CS-CLM-YYYY-NNNNN)
- A clear description of the issue and what outcome you expect
- Copies of supporting documents
