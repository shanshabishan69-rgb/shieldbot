# Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I buy a policy?
Through any branch, a registered Ceylon Shield agent, or the Shield App (motor, health and travel only). Life policies require a meeting with an agent and, in some cases, a medical examination.

### Where can I find my digital insurance card (e-card)?
In the Shield App under **My Policies → View e-card**. The e-card is accepted at all network hospitals and by the police as proof of motor insurance.

### Can foreigners buy insurance from Ceylon Shield?
Foreign nationals living in Sri Lanka with a valid residence visa can buy motor, health and home insurance. Travel Guard is only for Sri Lankan residents travelling overseas.

### How do I change my address or phone number?
Update it in the Shield App under **Profile**, or visit any branch with your NIC.

### Can I change the beneficiary of my life policy?
Yes, at any time. Submit a new Nomination Form at a branch or in the Shield App.

### What is the free-look period?
A period after you receive your policy document during which you can cancel for a full refund: **21 days for Life** and **14 days for Health**. See `policy_cancellation_and_refunds.md`.

### Do you offer Islamic (Takaful) insurance?
No, Ceylon Shield does not currently offer Takaful products.

### Is Ceylon Shield regulated?
Yes. Ceylon Shield Insurance PLC is licensed and regulated by the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL).

### I lost my policy document. What should I do?
Download a copy from the Shield App, or request a certified duplicate at any branch for a fee of LKR 500.

### Can I transfer my motor policy when I sell my vehicle?
Yes. The new owner must apply for the transfer within **14 days** of the change of ownership. The No Claim Bonus belongs to the original policyholder and is **not** transferred to the new owner.
