# Life Secure Plans — Product Terms

Life Secure offers two plans: **Term Protect** (pure protection) and **Smart Saver** (protection plus savings).

## 1. Term Protect

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Pays a lump sum to your beneficiaries if you die during the policy term |
| Policy terms | 10, 15, 20 or 25 years |
| Sum assured | LKR 1,000,000 to LKR 50,000,000 |
| Entry age | 18 to 60 years |
| Maximum age at end of term | 70 years |
| Maturity benefit | None (no payout if you survive the term) |

## 2. Smart Saver

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Life cover plus a savings payout at the end of the term |
| Policy terms | 10, 15 or 20 years |
| Entry age | 18 to 55 years |
| Minimum annual premium | LKR 36,000 |
| Maturity benefit | Sum assured + accumulated annual bonuses |
| Death benefit | Sum assured + bonuses accumulated up to the date of death |

**Bonus rate:** Bonuses are declared every year and are **not guaranteed**. The bonus rate declared for 2025 was **7.5%** of the sum assured. Past bonus rates do not guarantee future rates.

## 3. Optional Riders

| Rider | Benefit | Waiting Period |
|---|---|---|
| Critical Illness (CI) | Lump sum up to LKR 5,000,000 on first diagnosis of one of 30 listed critical illnesses (e.g., cancer, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure) | **90 days** from policy start |
| Accidental Death Benefit (ADB) | Pays an extra amount equal to the sum assured if death is caused by an accident | None |
| Hospital Cash | LKR 5,000 per day of hospitalisation, up to 30 days per year | 30 days for illness; none for accidents |
| Waiver of Premium | Future premiums are waived if you become permanently disabled | 6 months |

A **survival period of 30 days** after diagnosis applies to Critical Illness claims.

## 4. Key Policy Conditions

- **Free-look period:** 21 days from receiving the policy document. See `policy_cancellation_and_refunds.md`.
- **Grace period:** 30 days for late premium payment. If premiums remain unpaid after the grace period, the policy lapses.
- **Contestability period:** 2 years. If material facts (such as health conditions or smoking status) were not disclosed, Ceylon Shield may decline a claim made within 2 years of the policy start date.
- **Beneficiaries (nominees):** You can name or change beneficiaries at any time using the Nomination Form at any branch or in the Shield App.

## 5. Exclusions (Death Benefit)

1. Death resulting from taking part in a criminal act.
2. Death due to war or active military service.
3. Death related to a hazardous occupation or hobby that was not declared.
4. Non-disclosure of material facts within the contestability period.

Premium rates are listed in `life_premium_table.md`. Claims follow `life_claims_procedure.md`.
