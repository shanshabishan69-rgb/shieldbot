# Life Secure — Premium Rates

## 1. Term Protect — Annual Premium per LKR 1,000,000 Sum Assured (20-year term)

| Age at Entry | Non-smoker (LKR) | Smoker (LKR) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 2,400 | 3,900 |
| 30 | 2,900 | 4,700 |
| 35 | 3,700 | 6,100 |
| 40 | 5,100 | 8,600 |
| 45 | 7,300 | 12,400 |
| 50 | 10,800 | 18,500 |

- For 10-year and 15-year terms, multiply by **0.85**. For 25-year terms, multiply by **1.15**.
- **Example:** A 30-year-old non-smoker wanting LKR 10,000,000 cover for 20 years pays 2,900 × 10 = **LKR 29,000 per year**.

## 2. Rider Premiums (Annual)

| Rider | Premium |
|---|---|
| Critical Illness | LKR 1,800 per LKR 1,000,000 of CI cover |
| Accidental Death Benefit | LKR 600 per LKR 1,000,000 |
| Hospital Cash | LKR 4,500 flat |
| Waiver of Premium | 4% of the base premium |

## 3. Smart Saver

Smart Saver premiums are calculated individually from the chosen sum assured, term and age. The **minimum annual premium is LKR 36,000**. Ask an agent or visit a branch for a personalised illustration.
